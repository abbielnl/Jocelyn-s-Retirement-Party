"She never planned to teach — but teaching was always her calling."

Today we celebrate Jocelyn Murimi Kimemba Kirimi — a woman whose life has been a quiet, powerful testament to love, service, and sacrifice. She did not set out to be a teacher. Teaching found her, and she fell in love with it — and from that moment, she gave it everything she had.

Today I celebrate you and the incredible woman you are. Thank you for the many sacrifices you have made for our family, for the countless prayers you have lifted on our behalf, and for the love and guidance that continue to shape our lives every day. Your strength, patience, and faith have been a pillar for all of us.

As a teacher, you have moulded young minds and inspired so many to believe in themselves. As a mother and grandmother, you have nurtured generations with wisdom, warmth, and unconditional love. As a wife, you have served with humility, compassion, and devotion. Your life is truly a blessing to everyone who knows you.

Beyond the classroom, Jocelyn has always gone further — reaching into her own pocket and heart to support students facing hardship, whether struggling with school fees, family difficulties, or personal battles. No student was ever too broken, too poor, or too lost for her to help. She never asked about their background. She simply showed up — selflessly, consistently, and with grace.

Her Journey in Education

Decades of dedication, one chapter at a time

The Beginning
Matungulu Girls' High School
Teaching came calling, and Jocelyn answered. She joined Matungulu Girls' High School where she taught Geography and Religious Education for 6 formative years — discovering along the way that shaping young lives was not just a job, but her true purpose.
18 Years
Tala Boys' High School
Her longest and most defining chapter. For 18 remarkable years at Tala Boys', Jocelyn poured herself into her students — teaching, mentoring, and quietly changing the trajectories of countless young men. She became a fixture of the school's soul.
Post-COVID
Guidance & Counselling — A New Calling
When the world was shaken by the COVID-19 pandemic and students returned to school carrying invisible wounds, Jocelyn stepped forward. She took on the role of Guidance Counsellor — becoming a safe harbour for students navigating trauma, family hardship, and personal struggles. True to her nature, she also went beyond her role, quietly helping students in need of school fees or any support she could offer.
12½ Years
Eastleigh High School
In what would become her final and most cherished chapter, Jocelyn joined Eastleigh High School where she has taught for over twelve and a half years. Here she deepened her impact — not just as a Geography and R.E. teacher, but as a counsellor, a mother figure, and a fierce advocate for every student who walked through her door. It is here, surrounded by the students and colleagues who love her, that she takes her well-earned bow.
25 June 2026
A Well-Deserved Retirement 🎓
After years of tireless service, Jocelyn takes her final bow from the classroom — carrying the gratitude of every student she has ever touched.
